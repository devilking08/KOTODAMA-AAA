# Game Design Document — KOTODAMA: Echoes of the Nameless

> The world forgot its name. You remember.

---

## 1. Vision

A third-person action game that feels like the *Sekiro* school of melee —
posture, parry, deathblow — but is fully playable in a browser tab, with a
cohesive dark-fantasy story about names, memory and silence. Every system
exists to serve one loop:

> **Read the enemy. Answer with the right gesture. Take the opening.**
> (The same loop applies to the story: read the world, answer it, take your
> ending.)

Design pillars:

1. **Readability over spectacle.** Every attack is a promise: windup →
   strike → recover, in that order, every time. Posture is the health bar
   that matters.
2. **The world is the reward.** Exploration pays in memories, codex lore and
   quest entanglement — not just stat upgrades.
3. **Attention is the difficulty.** The true ending is not locked behind a
   skill check; it is locked behind *paying attention* to Ame, to the world,
   to yourself.
4. **One authored voice.** Nothing is generated by an LLM at runtime; every
   line is written for this world (though the *code* is AI-assisted, the
   *fiction* is fixed).

---

## 2. Core loop

```
Explore region ──► Find shrine (checkpoint / travel / save)
    │
    ├──► Talk to NPCs ──► Dialogue choices ──► quests / flags / ending weight
    ├──► Find memories / secrets ──► codex + side quest progress
    ├──► Fight enemies ──► posture → deathblow ──► XP / essence
    └──► Face chapter boss ──► outro ──► next chapter
```

Interleaved: the player can ignore the main thread and the world still has
narrative (side quests change flags that endings read).

## 3. Combat

### 3.1 The two bars

| Bar | What it means | Recoveries |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Health | The actual lifebar. When it hits 0 you die. | Rest at shrine, gourd, incense |
| Posture | Composure. Hitting, being hit, guarding, and option pressure drain it. At max, you are staggered. | Recovers over time when untouched, faster when guarding is *not* the answer (see below) |

Enemies work identically: **break posture → deathblow**. On grunts the
deathblow kills; on bosses it removes 16% max HP and often forces a phase.

### 3.2 Verbs

| Verb | Input | Function |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Light attack | J / LMB | Fast, chains, low posture damage |
| Heavy attack | H / Shift+LMB | Slower, high posture damage, guard-breaking |
| Guard | Q / RMB | Hold to block; tap *just* before impact = **perfect parry** |
| Dodge | Space | Brief i-frames; counts as a dodge even through perilous sweeps |
| Crouch | C | Reduces detection radius, enables stealth kills |
| Grapple | G | Vertical mobility to grapple points |
| Weapon art | F | Spends Resolve; unique per weapon; empowered by full Flow |
| Heal | R | Gourd (refills at shrines) |

### 3.3 Defense matrix

| Incoming | Guard | Parry | Dodge |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Normal slash | ✔ blocked (chip damage) | ✔ perfect (0 damage, +posture to enemy) | ✔ |
| Sweep (gold ring) | ✔ (blocked) | ✔ | ✔ |
| Perilous thrust (red ring) | ✘ (breaks guard) | ✘ | ✔ |
| Projectile | ✔ (facing) | ✘ | ✔ |
| Grab / AoE | ✘ (if unblockable) | ✘ | ✔ out of radius |

### 3.4 Flow and Resolve (the aggression economy)

- **Flow** builds from landing hits and perfect dodges (max 100). At 100
  your weapon art hits ~20% harder and consuming it counts toward the
  *In the Flow* achievement.
- **Resolve** (max 3, +1 via skill) is spent on weapon arts and gained from
  hits, parries, and deathblows.

### 3.5 Boss design

Each boss has 2–3 phases that remix the move pool, speed, aura hazards and
arena. All attacks telegraph: red ring = perilous, gold = sweep. Boss
"barks" are subtitled. Bosses are *learnable*, not stat-checked — posture
break windows are very generous by design (3.2s on bosses).

## 4. World & exploration

- **Regions** are large (460–760 m) and curated by hand-placed landmarks on
  seeded terrain.
- **Shrines** = Dark-Souls bonfires: rest (heal/refill), travel, save.
- **Memories** (blue light motes) are collectible lore — they double as side
  quest fuel.
- **Secrets** are named, discoverable spots with real rewards.
- **NPCs** move through chapters (some appear, some vanish) and remember you.

---

## 5. Progression

- **XP → levels → skill points**: three trees — Blade (offense/posture),
  Wind (parry/dodge/mobility/stealth), Spirit (resources/essence).
- **Weapons**: five, unlocked by story; all are viable; mastery is about
  switching for the fight.
- **Items**: incense (heal), talisman (attack buff), smoke bomb (escape),
  snow lotus (+max HP), dune glass (+max posture), bell tokens (vend),
  shrine seeds (grow shrines).

## 7. Endings

| Ending | Trigger | Tone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Speak | Choose to speak the world's name | Restoration; bittersweet |
| Silence | Let the silence finish | Quiet acceptance; melancholic |
| Become | Take the crown | Responsibility; heavy |
| Remember (true) | `true_ending_unlocked` (Ame's name + 6 side quests) and choose to speak your own name | Cathartic; uses the whole story |

The plural endings are *read*, not gated: `pickEnding(flags)` and
`trueEndingAvailable(flags, completedSideQuests)`.

---

## 8. Difficulty / accessibility

- **Story / Warrior / Nameless** adjust incoming/outgoing damage multipliers
  (0.55/1.0/1.55 taken; 1.15/1.0/0.88 dealt) — posture math stays.
- Parry/dodge windows widen slightly on Story.
- Options: quality tiers, bloom, film grain, outlines, Kurosawa B&W,
  colorblind filters, subtitle size/background, reduced motion,
  photosensitivity-safe flashes, hold-vs-toggle, game speed, touch auto.
- All UI is DOM + ARIA-labeled; keyboard navigable; no reliance on color
  alone for telegraphs (shape + ring also carry meaning).
